# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contribution Compass is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or personal characteristics.

We value careful evidence, respectful disagreement, curiosity, and generosity toward contributors
and the maintainers of projects represented in the catalog.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Give context, evidence, and reproducible examples where possible.
- Respect project boundaries, maintainer decisions, and contributor time.
- Accept correction gracefully and help repair harm.
- Avoid pressuring upstream maintainers or treating a recommendation as an entitlement to work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- sustained disruption, trolling, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or deliberately misleading claims;
- spam, coordinated pressure, or unsolicited promotion; and
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where
someone is representing Contribution Compass. It also applies when interacting with maintainers or
contributors of projects surfaced by the catalog.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential report, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/amirimi/contribution-compass/security/advisories/new) and
prefix the title with `[CONDUCT]`. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

The maintainer will review reports in context, protect privacy where practical, and choose a
proportionate response. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction,
removal of content, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Deliberately false or retaliatory
reports may themselves violate this code.
